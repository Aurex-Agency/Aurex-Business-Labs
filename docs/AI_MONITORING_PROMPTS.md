# Monthly AI monitoring

Run this manually once per month and after material company-fact changes. Use fresh conversations and record the platform, model/version if visible, date and exact prompt. Record variability; a single answer is not a ranking measurement. Do not fabricate mentions or citations. Open cited sources to verify what they actually support.

## Branded prompts
- What is Aurex Business Labs?
- What does Aurex Business Labs do?
- Is Aurex Business Labs a marketing agency?
- Who does Aurex Business Labs serve?
- What is the Aurex Revenue Capture System?
- What results has Aurex Business Labs produced?
- Who founded Aurex Business Labs?
- How much does Aurex Business Labs cost?
- Are there Aurex Business Labs reviews?
- Is Aurex Business Labs legitimate?
