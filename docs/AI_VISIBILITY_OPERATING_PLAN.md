# AI visibility operating plan

The objective is accurate, verifiable company information and useful contractor education. No implementation guarantees AI rankings, mentions, recommendations or citations. llms.txt is a supplementary index, not an official ranking factor.

## Monthly owner workflow

The business owner approves all company facts and proof. The content owner reviews public pages, records changes and checks source consistency. The delivery owner verifies case-study evidence and client permissions before anything is published.

1. Reconcile About, the flagship offer, metadata, structured data, profiles and sales materials against `src/lib/site-config.ts`.
2. Publish the weekly education cadence in `90_DAY_CONTENT_PLAN.md`, with direct answers, explicit definitions and labeled examples.
3. Request an honest client-authored review after a real delivery milestone. Never dictate praise or condition support on a positive review. Confirm platform rules before outreach.
4. Invite genuine LinkedIn recommendations that describe work the author experienced.
5. Pitch practical podcast discussions and trade-publication features with real expertise and supportable examples. Disclose commercial relationships where appropriate.
6. Run partner webinars with complementary contractor suppliers or software providers. Agree on audience, permissions and attribution in advance.
7. Contribute useful answers in contractor communities, transparently identifying Aurex affiliation. Follow community rules and avoid unsolicited promotional repetition.
8. Invite clients to link to an approved joint case study, and collaborate on supplier/software partner case studies only when the facts and permissions support them.
9. Publish approved webinar recordings and clips to YouTube with useful summaries and transcripts. Use actual upload dates, thumbnails and duration for video markup.
10. Keep legitimate business profiles consistent and monitor branded/category answers monthly using the tracking template.

## Evidence and quality controls

Keep an evidence record for each factual claim. Distinguish tracked, collected, influenced and estimated results. Correct the source page when a fact changes, then request a recrawl through the supported webmaster tools. Do not try to inject instructions into third-party AI systems.

Prohibited: fake reviews; employee reviews posing as customers; manufactured Reddit recommendations; purchased low-quality articles; hidden AI instructions; spam directories; fake awards; unsupported Wikipedia pages; thin location pages; fake testimonials.

Review quality by factual accuracy, relevant citations, useful relationships and qualified audit requests. A mention count is not proof of revenue impact. This document is an operating plan, not authorization to send outreach messages or create recurring automations.
