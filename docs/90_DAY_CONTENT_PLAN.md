# 90-day authority content plan

Publish after editorial approval. Only the first three cornerstone articles are implemented now. Do not publish thin drafts just to meet a quota.

## Weekly output

Two strong articles; one webinar recap; three to five short clips from the webinar; one email; three social posts; one FAQ improvement; one updated sales asset. Obtain recording and client permissions before repurposing media. Use real session dates and transcripts.

## Editorial gate

Start with a direct answer. Name the reader's decision. Define terms, include a worked hypothetical example where useful, label assumptions, and link to the relevant offer or methodology. Have a subject-matter reviewer check formulas and unsupported claims. Record actual author, publication and update dates. Review current platform or legal information against authoritative sources before making specific claims.
